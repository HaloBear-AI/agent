# HaloBear Agent — Open Industry Repository

This is the public repository of HaloBear Agent, where we openly share the AI skills, resources, and knowledge that are reshaping our industry. Everything here is built from real-world practice and shared with one goal: to help the entire industry evolve, together.

## About this repository

Welcome to the open industry repository of HaloBear Agent.

We believe AI will fundamentally change how our industry works, and that progress happens faster when knowledge is shared. This repository is our contribution: a growing collection of AI skills, workflows, reference materials, and practical know-how, drawn from hands-on experience.

### What you'll find here

- **AI skills:** reusable agent skills and prompts ready to put to work
- **Resources:** templates, references, and tools
- **Knowledge:** industry insights, methods, and best practices

### Our mission

To push the industry forward by making AI capability accessible to everyone in it.

Contributions, feedback, and ideas are always welcome.
